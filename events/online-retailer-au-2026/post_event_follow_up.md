## Email templates by segment

All templates use `[First Name]`, `[Company]`, `[Sender Name]` as merge fields and a `[Booth note]` field for the one specific detail the rep remembers from the conversation — every send should have at least one real, specific detail or it reads as a mass email.

### 1. Hot Prospect — flagged "Immediate follow-up"

**Use for:** leads a rep specifically flagged onsite as high-intent (e.g., ASICS, Rebel Sport-tier retailers who asked for next steps).

**Subject line options:**
- Following up from Online Retailer, [First Name]
- Great meeting you at Booth F103
- [Booth note] — let's keep the conversation going

**Body:**

> Hi [First Name],
>
> It was great meeting you at Online Retailer last week — I really enjoyed hearing about [Booth note, e.g., "what you're building out for loyalty" or "the challenges with your current SMS platform"].
>
> You mentioned you wanted to take a closer look at how Attentive could help [Company]. I'd love to set up time this week to walk through it in more detail — happy to bring in whoever from our team would be most useful for that conversation.
>
> Do you have 20 minutes in the next few days?
>
> Best,
> [Sender Name]

---

### 2. Standard Booth Visitor — no flag, badge scanned

### 3. Actively Evaluating a Switch (competitor displacement)

**Use for:** leads who specifically mentioned frustration with Klaviyo, Cordial, Braze, or another platform during the booth conversation. Junel's team recap noted several brands at this event wanting to move off Klaviyo — this template is built for exactly that conversation.

**Subject line options:**
- What switching to Attentive actually looks like
- A real example: how Hey Bud moved off Klaviyo
- Following up on the platform conversation from Online Retailer

**Body:**

> Hi [First Name],
>
> Good talking through what's not working with [current platform] at Online Retailer. You're not alone — it came up a lot on the floor this year.
>
> One example that might be useful: Hey Bud, an Australian skincare brand, made the same move from Klaviyo to Attentive. They used two-tap™ sign-up to grow their list while collecting zero-party data like skin type, then built segments around skin concerns, purchase history, and engagement to make every message more relevant. Attentive's Customer Success team worked with them to keep refining performance from there.
>
> Happy to walk through what a move like that would actually look like for [Company] — migration, timeline, all of it.
>
> Best,
> [Sender Name]

## Reminder for whoever sends these

Per the Attentive brand voice guide: keep it direct and human, connect every line to an outcome, use contractions, and don't reach for "leverage," "seamless," "unlock," or "supercharge." Every send should read like it came from a specific person who remembers the actual conversation — not a template.
